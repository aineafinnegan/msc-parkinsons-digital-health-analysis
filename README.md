# Opportunities for Connected Health in the Parkinson’s Disease Lifecycle

Coursework project exploring how connected health approaches could improve Parkinson’s disease care across early detection, diagnosis, monitoring, and long-term management.

## Overview
This project examines the Parkinson’s disease care pathway in Ireland and explores where connected health technologies could improve continuity of care. The report focuses on challenges such as delayed diagnosis, fragmented follow-up, limited specialist capacity, and difficulties in monitoring symptom fluctuations over time.

A central theme of the project is the potential of digital biomarkers, particularly speech-based biomarkers, to support earlier detection, more objective assessment, and continuous monitoring throughout the Parkinson’s lifecycle.

## Key focus areas
- Parkinson’s disease lifecycle and stages of care
- Challenges in the current Irish care pathway
- Opportunities for connected and continuous care
- Digital biomarkers for early detection and diagnosis
- Speech analysis as a tool for monitoring disease progression
- Remote monitoring of medication ON/OFF states
- Implementation barriers including privacy, accessibility, and digital infrastructure

## Project concept
As part of the assignment, a digital health concept called **DopaTrack** was proposed. The idea was an app-based platform using semantic and acoustic speech biomarkers to help patients and clinicians better identify and manage ON/OFF medication fluctuations in Parkinson’s disease.
